The Greenville Garden Club recently had the opportunity to present photos to the Bond County Fair Board of Directors, and the Board's president followed up with a kind note thanking the Club for the photos and for taking the time to make the presentation.

In response, the Greenville Garden Club is donating a 6' aluminum bench for the Midway, giving fairgoers a comfortable place to rest during the Bond County Fair.

![Greenville Garden Club members present a donation to the Bond County Fair Board](/images/uploads/BondCountyFairDonation.jpg)

Thank you to the Fair Board for the partnership, and we hope the new bench serves the community well for years to come.
