The grill was going strong at the Milk House this week for our latest fundraiser. Members and neighbors came out for brats, chicken, pork chops, and pork burgers, plus a table full of desserts made by our own members. Soda and chips rounded out the meal.

![A plate from the Milk House potluck fundraiser](/images/uploads/Potluck8-28%20(8).jpeg)

Every dollar raised goes straight into our scholarship program, helping us keep supporting local students who share our love of growing things. Thanks to everyone who came out, cooked, served, and ate — it all adds up.
