Members recently made the short drive south of Carlyle to visit Voss Pecan Farm — and it turns out we didn't have to go far to find the largest pecan-producing farm in the entire state of Illinois.

![Members arriving for the tour at Voss Pecan Farm](/images/uploads/vosspecan%20(1).JPG)

The tour took us through the working side of the operation, including the shellers and equipment that process the pecans once they're off the trees.

![Touring the shelling equipment at Voss Pecan Farm](/images/uploads/vosspecan%20(17).JPG)

From there we walked through the coolers, where the harvest is stored to stay fresh well past the growing season.

![Inside the coolers at Voss Pecan Farm](/images/uploads/vosspecan%20(16).JPG)

The best part of the afternoon, though, was the tasting table. We sampled several of the farm's products, and the sugar-and-cinnamon coated pecans were the clear favorite of the group.

![Sampling pecan products, including the sugar-and-cinnamon coated pecans](/images/uploads/vosspecan%20(9).JPG)

Thanks to everyone at Voss Pecan Farm for opening up the facility and sharing a little bit of what makes their operation the biggest in Illinois.

![A last look around Voss Pecan Farm before heading home](/images/uploads/vosspecan%20(21).JPG)
